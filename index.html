About the conference

ICHEP is the major biennial conference of the international particle physics community. In July 2028 it comes to Bangkok, hosted by Chulalongkorn University. Researchers from around the world will present the latest results in particle physics, astroparticle physics, cosmology, and accelerator and detector technology.

Plenary sessions

Reviews of the field's major results and open questions.

Parallel sessions

Talks on specific topics, from the Higgs and neutrinos to dark matter and new detector technologies.

Posters & outreach

Poster sessions for early-career researchers, plus public events in Bangkok.

Venue

Chulalongkorn University, founded in 1917, is Thailand's oldest university. Its campus is in Pathum Wan, in central Bangkok, and is easy to reach by BTS Skytrain and MRT.
